Agora · future

Design directions

Visual directions explored for the site, kept here so they can be revisited. Nothing on this page has shipped. Each palette uses the site's own CSS variables (--bg, --fg, --accent…), so applying one means replacing the :root blocks in frontend/index.html and frontend/canvas.html, plus the theme-color tags.

1Seasonal

A single theme for everyone (no light/dark switch) that changes with the Bay Area's seasons.

1.1Autumn

Explored October 2026. Started from muted red, oat and pumpkin, which read as soft and feminine; then moved toward workwear and leather tones for a more rugged take. Tobacco & ochre with earthy tags is the current favourite. Contrast is against each palette's background; text needs 4.5:1, buttons and large text 3:1.

1.2Winter

Explored October 2026. Bay Area winter is the rainy season, not snow: the hills turn green, storms come in off the Pacific, and persimmons, Meyer lemons and Dungeness crab are in season. All three use the earthy tag hues from autumn. Cypress & persimmon is the closest partner to tobacco & ochre, so it's the natural pick for a fall-to-winter transition; storm slate is the clearest break into winter.

2Logos

Logo explorations from October 2026, drawn on a design canvas. Each board shows the mark large, as an app icon, on the dark ground and at favicon size. All use the current palette (fog, bay navy, International Orange) and Big Shoulders Display. None has shipped; the site still uses the plain "Agora" wordmark.

2.1Marks

Eleven directions: the agora itself (stoa, theatron), a map pin, the Golden Gate (span, towers, tower A, above the fog), a crowd gathering in a circle, the fog sun, Victorian gables and a gull.

2.2Wordmarks

The two marks taken further, folded into the name: the bridge tower as the A, and the gathering circle as the o.
